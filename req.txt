Create a world-class modern e-commerce and agricultural brand website for **"Nusara Niaga"**, a modern Indonesian local rice and agricultural commerce brand that connects local farmers, rice mills, villages, and consumers.

Nusara Niaga starts with rice as its primary product, focusing on providing high-quality, fresh, affordable rice sourced from local farmers while strengthening the local village economy.

The brand philosophy is:

**"Dari Desa, Untuk Nusantara."**

Nusara Niaga represents a new generation of Indonesian agricultural commerce — combining local agricultural products, trusted village producers, modern technology, transparent supply chains, and convenient online shopping.

The website should feel:

* Modern
* Trustworthy
* Local
* Premium
* Warm
* Fresh
* Affordable
* Community-driven
* Professional
* Indonesian

Do NOT make the website look like a traditional grocery store.

It should look like a modern Indonesian agricultural startup and premium FMCG brand.
